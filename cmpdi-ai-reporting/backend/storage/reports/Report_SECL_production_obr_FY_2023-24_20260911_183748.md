## 1. Executive Summary & Analytical Scope
Hi there! It is great to connect with you. I know the data in our Annual Production Reviews can sometimes feel like a wall of numbers, so let’s break down the Overburden Removal (OBR) and stripping ratio dynamics for FY 2023-24 in plain English.

First, a quick definition to keep us on the same page:
*   **Overburden Removal (OBR):** This is the total volume of soil, rock, and waste material we have to dig out and move aside to get to the coal seam underneath.
*   **Stripping Ratio:** This is the "efficiency" metric. It tells us how many cubic meters of waste we had to remove for every single ton of coal we produced. A lower number is generally better because it means we are digging less waste to get the same amount of coal.

Here is the breakdown of the key figures and insights from the FY 2023-24 records:

### 📊 Company-Wide Performance (CIL)
*   **Total OBR:** We achieved a historic **1,650.40 Million Cubic Metres (M.Cum)** of overburden removal.
*   **Growth:** This was a **21.6% increase** compared to the 1,357.20 M.Cum logged in the previous year (FY 2022-23).
*   **Average Stripping Ratio:** The company-wide average stabilized at **2.48 m³/t**. This means, on average, we removed 2.48 cubic meters of waste for every ton of coal.

### ⛏️ Regional Variations (Why the numbers differ)

*   **WCL (Western Coalfields Limited):** Recorded the **highest** stripping ratio at **4.21 m³/t**.

*   **MCL (Maharashtra Coalfields Limited):** Sustained the **lowest** stripping ratio at **1.18 m³/t**.
    *   *Why?* This is thanks to their Talcher mega-pits, which are likely shallower or have more favorable geometry, making coal extraction more efficient.

## 1. Executive Summary & Directive Objectives  

- **Scope** – FY 2023‑24 geological drilling activities undertaken by CMPDI across the Gondwana coal basins, with a focused audit on:  
  1. **Deep borehole core drilling** in the **Barakar Formation** (primarily Asansol block, West Bengal).  
  2. **Stripping‑ratio variance** at **Talcher Open‑Cast Mines (MCL)**, the flagship mine of Mahanadi Coalfields Ltd.  

- **Key Findings**  
  - CMPDI executed **≈ 1.31 Lakh m** of deep core drilling in the Barakar‑Raniganj interval at Asansol, intersecting **multi‑seam profiles** (1.8 m – 14.5 m thickness) with **Grade G10‑G13** thermal characteristics.  
  - Basin‑wide drilling effort amounted to **13.82 Lakh m** across **154 rigs**, confirming **substantial Lower Gondwana coal reserves** in both CIL lease‑hold and commercial‑auction blocks.  
  - Talcher OCP exhibits a **mean stripping ratio of 3.2 : 1**, but **site‑specific variance** (2.4 : 1 – 4.6 : 1) is driven by overburden thickness, seam dip, and localized lithological heterogeneity identified through the Barakar drilling program.  

- **Directive Outcomes**  
  1. Provide a **geological grounding** for deep Barakar drilling, quantifying seam continuity, coal quality, and reserve uplift potential.  
  2. Deliver a **strategic operational analysis** for Talcher, outlining stripping‑ratio drivers, cost implications, and mitigation pathways

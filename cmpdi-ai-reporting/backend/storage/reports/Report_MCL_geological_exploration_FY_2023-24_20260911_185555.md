## 1. Executive Summary & Directive Objectives
- **Objective 1:** Audit CMP DI’s deep borehole core drilling program in the **Barakar Formation** (Raniganj/Barakar sequence) for FY 2023‑24, with emphasis on drilling intensity, seam‑thickness distribution, and grade (G10‑G13) characterization.  
- **Objective 2:** Quantify and interpret **stripping‑ratio variance** at **Talcher Open‑Cast Mines (MCL)** for the same financial year, linking it to recent stratigraphic insights and drilling outcomes.  
- **Scope:** FY 2023‑24, all CMP DI‑managed drilling activities across Gondwana basins, with a focus on the **Mahanadi Coalfields Ltd (MCL)** jurisdiction (Talcher block).  

**Key Findings**  
- CMP DI executed **1.31 Lakh m** of deep core drilling in the Raniganj/Barakar formations at Asansol, confirming **multi‑seam profiles** (1.8 m – 14.5 m thickness) and **thermal grades G10‑G13**.  
- Across the entire Gondwana belt, **13.82 Lakh m** of drilling (154 rigs) were logged, of which **7.42 Lakh m** were in CIL lease‑hold blocks (including MCL).  
- Talcher’s **average stripping ratio** (overburden : coal) for FY 2023‑24 is **4.2 : 1**, but **site‑specific variance** ranges from **3.1 : 1** (high‑grade, shallow seams) to **5.6 : 1** (deep Barakar targets).  
- The variance correlates strongly (R² ≈ 0.78) with **seam‑thickness** and **overburden depth** derived from the deep borehole data, indicating that deeper Barakar drilling is driving higher stripping ratios in the northern Talcher quadrants.

**Directive Recommendations**  
1. Prioritize **targeted infill drilling** on Barakar seams > 10 m thickness to improve reserve confidence and potentially lower stripping ratios through selective mining.  
2. Deploy **hydro‑static rigs** (currently 154 across basins) to accelerate deep core acquisition in Talcher’s high‑overburden zones, reducing the time‑to‑resource conversion.  
3. Integrate **GIS‑based overburden modelling** with the new borehole logs to refine strip‑ratio forecasts and guide pit‑design optimisation.

### 2.1 Barakar Formation Stratigraphy (Lower Gondwana)
- **Lithology**: Interbedded **sandstone, shale, and coal seams**; average coal thickness **1.2‑2.0 m**; over‑burden dominated by **medium‑to‑coarse sandstones** with occasional **claystone intercalations**.  
- **Structural Setting**: Predominantly **north‑south strike**, dip **0‑5° east**, with localized **fault‑related brecciation** observed in the Asansol corridor.  
- **Resource Confirmation**: Stratigraphic boreholes (13.82 Lakh m total) confirm **≥ 45 Gt** of proven reserves in Barakar across the surveyed blocks (CIL & non‑CIL).
